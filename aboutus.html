Our Legacy

Our Story

Celebrating Tradition and Excellence

Malam COC is a prestigious annual dinner that honors the spirit of Melayu Klasik, blending the grandeur of traditional Malay royal heritage with the vibrancy of contemporary corporate culture. It is a night where elegance meets legacy, fostering unity and pride among our team.
